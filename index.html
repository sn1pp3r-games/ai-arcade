    Map

    Quick tutorial

    1. Move the view — The camera is not locked. Use W A S D or the arrow keys to pan. Drag with Alt, right mouse, or middle mouse. Move the cursor to the edge of the map to scroll. Mouse wheel zooms in and out.
    2. Build & inspect — Choose a tool, then click or drag on grass to place. With no tool selected, click any built tile to see lifetime revenue and upkeep. Tap i on build rows for blueprints. Connect paths from the gate to rides and shops.
    3. Run the park — Adjust ticket price (higher earns more but can hurt satisfaction). Watch guests, satisfaction, and net / min. Pause or change speed in the header. Use the minimap (corner) to see where you are.
